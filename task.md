## Design Context

### Users
This is a portfolio piece for people who want a capable, straightforward fitness companion. The experience should support both sides of the workflow equally: building a useful workout or program, and logging/reviewing progress over time. The interface should make those tasks feel approachable without assuming a narrow level of training expertise.

### Brand Personality
Calm, confident, refined, and highly usable. The product should feel trustworthy and premium without becoming flashy or over-designed. It should help the user focus on training decisions rather than impress them with unnecessary visual noise.

### Aesthetic Direction
Keep it simple and minimal, but elegant. Use a clean premium studio aesthetic: warm off-white or soft stone backgrounds, deep charcoal/ink text, subtle taupe or muted olive accents, and generous whitespace. Pair a refined serif for major headings with a modern sans for labels and body copy. Prioritize quiet luxury over novelty—simple layout, thoughtful typography, soft borders, and careful spacing. The interface should feel functional first, polished second, and never cluttered. Avoid chatbot clichés, neon UI, heavy gradients, glassmorphism, oversized decorative elements, and busy card-heavy layouts. Light mode is preferred and should feel calm rather than sterile.

### Design Principles
- Prioritize clarity, calm, and usability over decoration.
- Use restrained color, typography, and spacing to create elegance without excess.
- Keep the interface clean and functional: every element should support the workout flow or the coaching task.
- Let whitespace and hierarchy do the work; avoid unnecessary visual noise.
- Maintain strong accessibility and responsiveness while preserving a polished, premium feel.
